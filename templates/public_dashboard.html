How the E-Voting System Works

Step 1: Verification
Ensure your name is registered on the Electoral Roll. You will receive an official notification containing your Secure Authorization PIN.

Step 2: Authentication
Navigate to the Secure Voting Portal. Enter your Registered Email Address and your 6-character Authorization PIN to verify your identity.

Step 3: Casting the Ballot
Review the list of officially registered candidates on the digital ballot paper. Select your preferred candidate and click "Cast Vote Securely".

Step 4: Cryptographic Sealing
Once cast, your vote is cryptographically separated from your identity. The system permanently records your vote in the digital ballot box while ensuring absolute anonymity. Double voting is technically impossible.

General Guidelines
  • Voting is a fundamental democratic right and a civic duty.
  • Do not share your Authorization PIN with anyone. The Election Commission will never ask for your PIN.
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection before entering the polling booth.
  • Impersonation or attempt to vote multiple times is a federal offense punishable by strict imprisonment.
